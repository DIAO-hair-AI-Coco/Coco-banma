课文学习 · 活塞与连杆READING — PISTON AND CONNECTING ROD

📖 课文 1 · 活塞的作用 Piston Functions

The piston is a cylindrical shaped hollow part, it moves up and down inside the engine cylinder. The piston absorbs heat from the gas and this heat must be carried away if the metal temperature is to be held within safe limits.
活塞是一个在发动机气缸中上下移动的中空的圆筒状塞子。活塞从气体中吸热，假如要使金属的温度保持在安全限度以内，就必须散热。

📖 课文 2 · 活塞组件 The Components of a Piston

The piston consists of piston head, piston rings, piston lands, piston skirt and piston pinhole. The piston head is the top surface against which the explosive force is exerted. The piston rings are installed in grooves. There are two types of rings, compression rings and oil-control rings. The piston lands are parts of piston between the ring grooves, it provides a seating surface for the sides of piston rings. The piston skirt forms a bearing area in contact with the cylinder wall. The piston pinhole in the piston is used to connect the connecting rod.
活塞由顶部、活塞环、环槽岸、裙部和活塞销座孔组成。活塞顶部是活塞的上表面，燃烧所产生的爆炸力作用于此。活塞环安装在凹槽中，分为两种：气环和油环。活塞环槽之间的部位称为环槽岸，为活塞环两端提供支承面。活塞裙部形成活塞与缸壁的接触支承面。活塞销座孔用来连接活塞和连杆。

📖 课文 3 · 连杆 Connecting Rod

The connecting rod is attached to the crankshaft at big end and to the piston at the small end. And it must be strong and rigid and light in weight as well. The connecting rod is composed of connecting rod small end, connecting rod shank, connecting rod big end, connecting rod cap and connecting rod bearing half shells.
连杆大头与曲轴相连，小头与活塞配合。它必须有足够的强度和刚度，并且质量要尽可能轻。连杆由小头、杆身、大头、连杆盖和轴瓦组成。
🔍 记忆要点：活塞 = 顶 + 环 + 环槽岸 + 裙 + 销座孔；活塞环分气环（compression ring）与油环（oil-control ring）；连杆 = 小头 + 杆身 + 大头 + 连杆盖 + 轴瓦。

课文学习 · 燃油系统 READING — FUEL SYSTEM

📖 课文 1 · 燃油系统的功用 Function of the Fuel System

The function of both the carburetor fuel system and the fuel injection system is to supply a combustible mixture of air and fuel to the engine. A gasoline fuel system draws fuel from the tank and forces it into the fuel-metering device (carburetor, gasoline injectors), using either a mechanical (engine-driven) or electric fuel pump.
化油器燃油系统和燃油喷射系统的共同功用，都是向发动机供给可燃混合气。汽油机燃油系统从油箱吸取燃油，利用机械式（发动机驱动）或电动式燃油泵加压，把燃油送入燃油计量装置（化油器、汽油喷射器）。

📖 课文 2 · 燃油供给系统的基本部件 Basic Parts of a Fuel Supply System

Fuel Tank: stores gasoline. Fuel Pump: draws fuel from the tank and forces it to the fuel-metering device. Fuel Filter: removes contaminants in the fuel. Fuel Line: carries fuel between the tank, the pump, and other parts.
油箱：储存燃油。燃油泵：从油箱吸取燃油并加压送入燃油计量装置。燃油滤清器：滤除燃油中的杂质（污染物）。油管：在油箱、油泵与其他部件之间输送燃油。

📖 课文 3 · EFI 的四个子系统 Four Subsystems of EFI

The more common type of timed fuel injection is the electronic-timed fuel injection, also known as electronic fuel injection (EFI). An EFI system can be divided into four subsystems: (1) Fuel delivery system; (2) Air induction system; (3) Sensor system; (4) Computer control system.
最常见的定时喷射是电子定时燃油喷射，又称电控燃油喷射（EFI）。电控燃油喷射系统可分为四个子系统：（1）燃油传输系统；（2）空气供给系统；（3）传感器系统；（4）计算机控制系统。

📖 课文 4 · 喷油器的工作原理 How the Fuel Injector Works

The fuel injector for an EFI system is a coil or solenoid-operated fuel valve. When not energized, the spring pressure keeps the injector closed, keeping fuel from entering the engine. When current flows through the injector coil, the magnetic field attracts the injector armature. The injector opens, squirting fuel into the intake manifold under pressure.
电控喷射系统的喷油器是一个由线圈/螺线管操纵的燃油阀。未通电时，弹簧压力使喷油器保持关闭，燃油不能进入发动机；当电流通过线圈时，磁场吸引喷油器电枢，喷油器打开，把燃油在压力下喷入进气歧管。

📖 课文 5 · 空气供给与传感器系统 Air Induction & Sensor System

The air induction system typically consists of a throttle valve, sensors, an air filter and connecting ducts. The throttle valve regulates how much air flows into the engine; in turn, it controls engine power output. When the pedal is depressed, the throttle valve swings open to allow more air into the engine. The sensor system monitors engine conditions and reports to the computer. A sensor changes circuit resistance or voltage with a change in a condition.
空气供给系统通常包括节气门、传感器、空气滤清器和连接导管。节气门调节进气量，进而控制发动机输出功率；踩下踏板时节气门开大，更多空气进入发动机。传感器系统监测发动机运行状态并反馈给控制器——传感器会随条件变化而改变电路电阻或电压。

总结 · 知识树SUMMARY — KNOWLEDGE TREE

🌳 知识树 Fuel System

⛽ 燃油系统 Fuel System
燃油供给系统 Fuel Supply
  • 燃油箱 fuel tank 储存燃油
  • 燃油泵 fuel pump 吸油加压
  • 燃油滤清器 fuel filter 滤除杂质
  • 油管 fuel line 输送燃油
  • 燃油压力调节器 pressure regulator 保持预设压力
EFI 四个子系统
  • 燃油传输 fuel delivery
  • 空气供给 air induction 节气门 throttle
  • 传感器系统 sensor system
  • 计算机控制 computer control
喷油器 Fuel Injector
  • 线圈/螺线管 coil / solenoid
  • 弹簧 spring 断电关闭
  • 电枢 armature 受磁场吸引
  • 针阀与喷孔 nozzle 喷油
典型传感器 Sensors
  • 氧传感器 oxygen sensor
  • 歧管压力传感器 manifold pressure
  • 节气门位置传感器 throttle position
  • 冷却液温度 / 进气温度传感器
  • 空气流量计 air flow meter
  • 曲轴位置 / 发动机转速传感器

📌 核心要点 Key Points

① 燃油系统的功用：供给可燃混合气 combustible mixture ② 供给系统 4 部件：油箱、油泵、滤清器、油管 ③ EFI = Electronic Fuel Injection 电控燃油喷射 ④ EFI 4 子系统：燃油传输、空气供给、传感器、计算机控制 ⑤ 压力调节器：压力足够时把多余燃油送回油箱 ⑥ 喷油器：断电弹簧关闭，通电磁场吸引电枢喷油 ⑦ 传感器：随温度/压力/位置变化改变电阻或电压
